# Comics United - Beta Testing Guide

## 🧪 Welcome Beta Testers!

Thank you for participating in the Comics United beta! Your feedback is crucial for building the best networking platform for comedians.

## 🎯 What We're Testing

### Core Features
- **Authentication System** - Sign up/login process
- **User Interface** - Glass-morphism design and responsiveness  
- **Comedian Profiles** - Profile browsing and verification system
- **Venue Reviews** - Venue discovery and review functionality
- **Messaging System** - Private communication between comedians
- **Collaboration Rooms** - Group spaces for projects and discussions

### Beta Focus Areas
1. **User Experience** - How intuitive is the interface?
2. **Performance** - Loading times and responsiveness
3. **Mobile Experience** - How well does it work on phones/tablets?
4. **Feature Requests** - What's missing that you'd love to see?
5. **Bug Discovery** - Any errors or unexpected behavior?

## 📝 How to Give Feedback

### Method 1: In-App Feedback (Recommended)
1. Look for the orange **"BETA VERSION"** banner at the top
2. Click **"Share Feedback"** button
3. Fill out the comprehensive feedback form
4. Submit your thoughts!

### Method 2: Footer Feedback
- Scroll to the bottom of any page
- Click **"Give Feedback"** in the footer

### What to Include in Your Feedback
- **Rating** (1-5 stars) of your overall experience
- **Category** of feedback (UI, Performance, Features, etc.)
- **Detailed comments** about what you liked/disliked
- **Suggestions** for improvements
- **Bug reports** if you encounter issues
- **Email** (optional) for follow-up questions

## 🐛 Bug Reporting Guidelines

When reporting bugs, please include:
- **What you were trying to do**
- **What happened vs. what you expected**
- **Steps to reproduce the issue**
- **Your device/browser information** (automatically captured)
- **Screenshots if applicable**
